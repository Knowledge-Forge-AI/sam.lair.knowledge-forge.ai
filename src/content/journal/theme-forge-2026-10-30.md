STTN 0.4.0 and CTTN 0.1.0 bring the Terminal Nova visual language to documentation and Tailwind/shadcn components. I want the shared design to remain recognizable without hiding the difference between their consumers.

## What this release means to me

The first stable CTTN release does not turn every browser or assistive-technology scenario into a proven result. Deterministic theme output is useful evidence, with its own clear scope.

## The release boundary

STTN regenerates Forge Console with Loom 0.5.0 and qualifies its graphics with Burst 0.7.0. CTTN provides light/dark tokens, cyan actions, amber accents, status labels and compiled JavaScript/declarations without added runtime dependencies.
