I prepared APGR 0.13.1 to correct an important packaging mismatch: the Apple Silicon wheel advertised macOS 11, while its binary required macOS 12. A compatibility label has to describe the executable inside it.

## What this release means to me

The correction gets its own version. I want previously published assets to keep their identities, and I want wheel metadata and native executable inspection to agree before an installation claim is made.

## The release boundary

Packaging checks now bind the Mach-O support minimum to the wheel tag. Linux targets remain unchanged, as do the forty-six-skill corpus and dispatcher lineage. Tagged notes retain candidate-era wording; the published release and attestations establish release identity.
