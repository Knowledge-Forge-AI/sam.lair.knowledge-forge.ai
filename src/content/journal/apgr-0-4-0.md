APG 0.4.0 expands the catalog to twenty-eight skills. I gave manager routing a distinct surface because deciding what work should happen, assigning it and accepting its result are different responsibilities.

## What this release means to me

The nine new capabilities remain provisional. I can make a useful capability public without pretending that publication has completed the work of qualifying it.

## The release boundary

The infrastructure also gains Python-first operational reports, a pytest-based test architecture and version-aware release validation. Those pieces make a recorded result easier to inspect and a public candidate easier to reproduce.
