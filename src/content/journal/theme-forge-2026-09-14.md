I expanded Burst into versioned vector scenes and Loom into a five-layer Theme v2 model. The coordinated Terminal Nova and Nebular releases connect those engines to a theme consumer and desktop work areas.

## What this release means to me

The family can share design intent without sharing one package contract. I keep the macOS developer-app limit, the SVG-only formula and the recorded build-tool exposure visible in the release story.

## The release boundary

Burst 0.5.0 adds deterministic scene compilation, bounded import and accessibility metadata. Loom 0.2.0 adds semantic catalog identity and local recompilation of exchanged candidates. Terminal Nova 0.2.0 adopts the catalog component architecture; Nebular 0.3.0 binds exact engine inputs.
