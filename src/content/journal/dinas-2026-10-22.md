DINAS was the original inspiration for RepoMap. Maintaining a modular workstation made the relationships between shell wrappers, Nix modules, applications and source selection impossible to treat as a simple list of files.

## What this release means to me

I wanted a map that could explain those connections and point back to evidence. RepoMap carries that idea into repository analysis; the move toward Common Lore Index keeps the origin while giving the broader direction a name.

## The release boundary

The v0.0.1 Core preview is a curated source snapshot with example identities. The composition preview preserves adapter registrations and ownership contracts, but some adapters still require authorized access. These previews do not provide a minimal unattended installer or establish host activation.
