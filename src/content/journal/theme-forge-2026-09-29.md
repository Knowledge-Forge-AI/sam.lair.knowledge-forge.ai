Burst 0.6.1, Loom 0.4.0 and Solar Sail 0.2.1 advance independently on this release day. I want those identities to stay explicit even when the family’s work is coordinated.

## What this release means to me

A compiler update does not replace an older compiler already bundled inside a desktop application. Keeping that distinction visible makes it easier to understand what a particular installed workbench actually contains.

## The release boundary

The published records preserve frozen artifacts and producer provenance for each component. Exact changes belong to each version’s source and changelog rather than a presumed family-wide version number.
