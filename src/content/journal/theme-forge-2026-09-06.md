Burst 0.4.0 and Nebular Fusion 0.1.0 bring the engine and desktop workbench together. I wanted a place to review local design artifacts while keeping the compiler’s own source and package identity independently usable.

## What this release means to me

The earliest workbench is a macOS developer application. Its presence does not imply Linux availability, notarization or completion of every package channel. Those are separate milestones in this journal.

## The release boundary

Burst supplies brand-system and design-evidence capabilities; the Tauri shell provides a local review surface. Burst 0.4.0’s release distinguishes the source-composition archive from its npm distribution package; similar archive names are not interchangeable artifacts.
