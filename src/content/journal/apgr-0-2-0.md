With APG 0.2.0, I promoted the original six process skills to stable within their documented scope. The other half of that work was delivery: a skill people cannot adopt, verify or roll back predictably is difficult to trust.

## What this release means to me

I want maturity to describe evidence about a particular practice. It should never become a general permission slip for an agent, or a suggestion that every task needs the entire catalog.

## The release boundary

A deterministic public-release builder checks projected source and append-only lineage. Managed lifecycle operations make adoption, verification and rollback explicit, and the release detects the omitted-wrapper problem in the first public version.
